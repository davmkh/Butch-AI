## What we turn in

Only two documents go to the instructor: the sprint backlog
([SPRINT_2_BACKLOG.md](SPRINT_2_BACKLOG.md)) and the sprint report
([SPRINT_2_REPORT.md](SPRINT_2_REPORT.md)). Sprint 2 planning, execution, review, and

files in this folder (the product backlog, the UML pages, the Kanban notes) are working references.

## Demo video outline

Every member appears and talks about their own contribution. Show the running app, not
screenshots (sprint 1 feedback).

| Section                         | Time      | What to cover                                                          |
| ------------------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Introduction                    | 1–2 min   | The team, the project, the sprint 2 objectives                         |
| Sprint backlog overview         | 2–3 min   | The updated backlog items and who owns them                            |
| Kanban board walkthrough        | 2–3 min   | An item moving from To Do to Done on the live board                    |
| Feature demonstration           | 5–7 min   | New or improved features, and the changes made after sprint 1 feedback |
| Responsible use of AI           | 1.5–2 min | Which tools, what for, limits, and how we validated the output         |
| Sprint review and retrospective | 1–2 min   | Outcomes, lessons, and plans for sprint 3                              |
| Conclusion                      | 1–2 min   | Overall progress and the roadmap for sprint 3                          |

The total is 13.5 to 21 minutes.
